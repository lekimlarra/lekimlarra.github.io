About

I'm a postdoctoral research fellow with a strong interest in adaptive immunity, particularly the biology of memory CD4⁺ T cells during viral infections.

My research focuses on understanding how tissue-resident memory T cells are generated and maintained in the lung, and how they contribute to long-term protection.

I hold a PhD in Biomedical Sciences and have experience with mouse models, flow cytometry, immunological assays and molecular biology techniques. I value interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific rigor and mentorship.

“I'm passionate about dissecting immune mechanisms and translating findings into meaningful insights for vaccine design and immunotherapy.”
