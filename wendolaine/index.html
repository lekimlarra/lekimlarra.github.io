I'm a postdoctoral researcher passionate about adaptive immunity, dissecting immune mechanisms to inform vaccine design and immunotherapy.

My research focuses on understanding how tissue-resident memory T cells are generated and maintained in the lung, and how they contribute to long-term protection.

I hold a PhD in Biomedical Sciences and have experience working with mouse models, flow cytometry, immunological assays and molecular biology techniques.

I value interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific rigor and mentorship.
